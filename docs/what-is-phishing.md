# What Is Phishing?

Phishing is a type of social engineering where an attacker pretends to be a trusted person or organisation to trick you into giving away information, such as passwords or bank details, or into installing malware.

## Why it works
- It creates urgency or fear ("Your account will be closed today").
- It looks like a real brand, colleague or service.
- It relies on people acting quickly without checking.

## What attackers want
| Goal | Example |
|---|---|
| Steal logins | A fake sign-in page for your email |
| Steal money | A fake invoice or payment request |
| Install malware | A harmful attachment or download link |
