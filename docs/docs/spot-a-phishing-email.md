# How to Spot a Phishing Email

## Warning signs
| Red flag | What to look for |
|---|---|
| Sender address | Slight misspellings or an unrelated domain |
| Urgency | "Act now" or "final warning" |
| Generic greeting | "Dear customer" instead of your name |
| Links | The real address (shown when you hover) does not match the text |
| Attachments | Unexpected files, especially zip or macro documents |
| Requests | Asks for a password, PIN or payment details |

## Fictional example
> From: it-support@company-helpdesk.example
> Subject: URGENT: Your mailbox is full
> Dear user, your mailbox will be deleted in 24 hours. Click here to verify your password now.

Red flags: unknown sender domain, urgency, generic greeting and a request for a password.

## Quick check before you click
- [ ] Do I know the sender, and was I expecting this?
- [ ] Does the link really go where it says?
- [ ] Is it asking for something sensitive or urgent?
